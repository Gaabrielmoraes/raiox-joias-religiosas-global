05Páginas institucionais

Estado: rascunho. Padrão observado: "30 Day Happiness Guarantee" com reposição ou reembolso e personalizado sem devolução por arrependimento (Sugar Spring), troca em 30 dias e garantia de cor (Lainly, Viole Grace), envio dos EUA em 5 a 7 dias úteis. As datas de order-by valem para envio dos EUA; com fornecedor na China, antecipar 2 semanas. E-mail provisório.
Contact Us (contact)

We are a small family-run gift studio and we read every message. Email hello@giveningrace.com and we reply within 24 hours, Monday to Friday. For gift orders, include the order number and the name on the card.

Shipping & Holiday Order-By Dates (shipping-policy)

Free shipping on every US order. Gift sets are printed and packed in 2 to 5 business days and delivered in 5 to 7 business days after that.

Order-by dates (standard shipping): Christmas: December 10. Easter: March 15. Mother's Day: April 26. First Communion: at least 3 weeks before the ceremony.

You can ship directly to the recipient: we never include the price.

30-Day Happiness Guarantee (refund-policy)

If anything is wrong with your gift, email a photo within 30 days of delivery and we will send a replacement or refund you, at our cost.

Because every card is printed with your words, we cannot accept returns for change of mind, and orders can be changed or cancelled within 24 hours of purchase. Please check the names on the card before ordering.

Color guarantee: if the plating fades or turns the skin green under normal wear, we replace it.

Privacy Policy (privacy-policy)

Given in Grace collects only what is needed to deliver your gift (names, address, email, the message on the card). We never sell your data and never show the recipient what you paid. Payments are processed by Shopify Payments. Ask us to delete your data any time at hello@giveningrace.com.

Terms of Service (terms-of-service)

By using giveningrace.com you agree to these terms. Prices are in USD. Jewelry is 316L stainless steel with PVD plating and imitation pearls or crystals unless stated otherwise. Colors may vary slightly on screen. These terms are governed by the laws of the United States.
